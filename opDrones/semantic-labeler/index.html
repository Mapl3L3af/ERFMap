From labels to learning

Export downloads a backup and trains your model on the erfMap server. When training completes, new analyses automatically use it. For paths, draw polygons around the full paved width and label adjacent grass separately. Include bends, junctions and shade. Lines teach only a 12 px strip; dots use a 6 px radius. Unmarked pixels are ignored. Training status appears below the image.
